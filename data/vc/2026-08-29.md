What that number actually means:

A high follow-on rate means fewer, deeper bets - not more logos on a portfolio page.
It compounds.
Every additional round is a decision to lean in again, at a higher price, with more on the line - made by people who'd already seen the company up close and chose to go further.

early

Translation:
Khosla Ventures doesn't just show up early. It shows up early, and then it keeps showing up - more than anyone else in the data.
